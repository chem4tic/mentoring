The approach

Money isn't a math problem. It's a habit problem.

Most advice about money assumes you're already stable — that you just need a slightly better strategy. But when you're in debt or living paycheck to paycheck, the problem usually isn't a lack of financial knowledge. It's the habits, decisions, and patterns built during years of just trying to get through the month.

"You don't need a finance degree. You need a plan you'll actually stick to — and someone who's been there to help you build it."

I'm not here to sell you investment products or manage your money — I'm not licensed to, and that's not what this is. This is about building a realistic budget, breaking unhelpful spending habits, getting out from under debt, and finally building the kind of savings that lets you breathe.

Looking honestly at your own finances is uncomfortable — it's well known what it's like to avoid it for years. It gets easier the moment you stop doing it alone.
