Your guide - Karol

Over a decade in corporate IT. The part I loved most was never the tech.

For more than ten years, I worked as a technical expert in a corporate IT environment — sitting with customers to manage their configurations, troubleshoot what wasn't working, and build the kind of ongoing relationship where they trusted me to have their back.

Internally, I helped build the standards that kept systems reliable, and verified that they held up to them. But out of everything I did, what I valued most was simpler: learning from the people around me, and passing that on to whoever needed it. That's what eventually pulled me toward mentoring — and it's why I use my own career as proof of what I tell my clients: you really can build something better from where you are.

Still on the fence? Book a free, 20-minute intro call — no pressure.

The approach

You don't need to become someone new. You need to see who you already are.

Most people don't lack potential — they lack proof of it, at least in words anyone outside their old job would recognize. "Ran the pass during dinner service" sounds like a shift. "Led a team through pressure, every night, without missing a beat" sounds like a skill any employer wants. Same experience, two very different ways of seeing it.

"Nothing was ever missing. It was just waiting to be uncovered."

I'm not here to sell you a new set of skills — you already have what you need. My job is to help you see it clearly, believe it, and turn it into a concrete plan for the leap you're considering.

Taking a leap is easier with a map. That's what the plan is for — practical next steps, not blind faith.
